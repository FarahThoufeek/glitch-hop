GLITCH HOP

HOW TO PLAY

🡆 Control the rocket using Left and Right Arrow Keys.

🡆 Dodge falling neon blocks. Colliding with one will end the game.

🡆 Collect stars (the yellow circles) to earn +5 points each.

🡆 As time passes, obstacles fall faster.

🡆 Expect heavy glitch effects, screen distortion, shake, and visual corruption. This is intentional — stay focused.
